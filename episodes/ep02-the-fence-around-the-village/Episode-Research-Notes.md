# Episode Research Notes — The Fence Around the Village (1962–1964)

**Where They Stood: Vietnam War · Episode 2**

## Episode overview

This episode examines what life inside South Vietnam’s Strategic Hamlet Program changed for rural residents between 1962 and 1964: home relocation, lost crops and bamboo cash reserves, household registration, curfew gates, differences across the Mekong Delta, the November 1963 military review, and the 1964 return to old settlements in Bến Tượng. Timecodes match the final English long-form cut (`7:06`, `426.77 s`).

## Primary sources

- **Official RVN booklet:** Republic of Vietnam, Directorate General of Information, *Viet Nam’s Strategic Hamlets* (Saigon, February 1963), pp. 3–14, 18, 22–24, 37–38.
- **Contemporary field survey:** [John C. Donnell and Gerald Cannon Hickey, *The Vietnamese “Strategic Hamlets”: A Preliminary Report*, RAND RM-3208-ARPA (September 1962)](https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_memoranda/RM3208.html), pp. iii–viii, 1–28.
- **Diplomatic & military records:** [Theodore Heavner’s letter relaying early survey observations in Vĩnh Long and Kiến Hòa, 19 March 1962 (FRUS 1961–1963, Vol. II, doc. 116)](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v02/d116); Third Tactical Zone Instruction on strategic and fighting hamlets, 16 March 1962 (cited in Phạm Đức Thuận 2017, pp. 63–64); Revolutionary Military Council minutes on the Strategic Hamlet Program, 16 November 1963 (National Archives Center II, cited in Phạm Đức Thuận 2017, p. 221).
- **Contemporary photography & archival 16mm film:** [Truman Library / Gardiner Papers, Củ Chi (3 March 1962, NARA NAIDs 348663363 & 348663381)](https://www.trumanlibrary.gov/photograph-records/2014-1951); [U.S. National Archives 16mm reel *Strategic Hamlet Draper* (NARA NAID 652988, 1963, Public Domain)](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/652988); U.S. National Archives reel, Tuy Hòa (NARA NAID 28773, 16 September 1962, Unrestricted); U.S. Army Signal Corps photographs (`111-CCV`: `CC22529`, `CC22534`, `CC22506`, `CC43419`, Public Domain).

## Evidence limitations

The RAND RM-3208 survey covered selected early pilot areas around Saigon, Vĩnh Long, Kiến Hòa, and Long An in January–April 1962 rather than the entire country. Contemporary government claims (official booklet, 10 April 1962 Bến Tượng report) and farmer interviews are attributed separately whenever figures conflict. Most NARA 652988 and Tuy Hòa 28773 sequences provide contemporary visual context (`ILLUSTRATIVE FILM`) rather than filming the exact villages named in written archives; specific verified locations (Lương Phú A, Tuy Hòa, Bàu Trai) are labeled on screen.
